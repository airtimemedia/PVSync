    What good lip sync looks like for your audio

    This is not your video. For every half second of your audio, we found the real recorded mouth, out of 28,000 speakers in our library, that the model thinks matches it best, and played them back to back. The face changes every half second; watch the lips, not the person.

    It isn't perfect. Each match is a half-second clip picked for the half second as a whole, not sound by sound, so individual mouth shapes inside it can be off. For a per-sound check, use the scores above.

    Videos are deleted within an hour of upload. Results are kept for an hour so this page can show them. Limit: a few tries per hour.
